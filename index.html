          From our notes

          Good

          to know
          Time zone

          Same time as Ottawa in April. No clock changes.

          Sundays, French side

          Most shops and boutiques close. Supermarkets open Sunday mornings only (~8 AM–12:45 PM). No shopping plans for Sun Apr 4.

          Sunset snack

          Crackers from home (Mary's or rice crackers) + chèvre from Super U.

          Grocery shuttle

          Someone said GCBC runs one. If so, we may skip a grocery stop on the way from the airport. Fee and schedule still to confirm.

          Post-breakfast fatigue · test over a few brunches
          • 15–20 g protein 15–30 min before a carb-heavy breakfast (bar with 15 g+ protein and low sugar, a shake, or two hard-boiled eggs)
          • Or eat protein and vegetables first, carbs last
          • A 10–15 min walk afterward
          • Or add eggs, or split the sweet dish and order something savoury

          These reliably cut the glucose spike. Whether the spike causes the fatigue is unconfirmed.
